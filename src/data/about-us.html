33 Black is alternative rock band from Reno, Nevada.

An unlikely alloy of artists like Gin Blossoms, Zach Bryan, and John Mayer: 33 Black delivers the raw sounds of guitar-driven alt-rock, the honesty of country lyricism, and the addicting riffs and melodies of modern pop heroes.
